Allocation Rules

  • Train length must not exceed platform length.
  • A platform cannot host trains during overlapping occupation intervals.
  • Occupation continues until departure plus the clearance buffer.
  • Automatic allocation considers trains in scheduled arrival order.
  • Trains without a suitable platform remain pending.
  • Cancelled allocations remain in the history.
